A chance to experience the game at another level ⚽️

Our 2014 boys have been given the opportunity to experience Barcelona through football, opening up a world of possibilities beyond their own pitch.

They’ll train in Spain, take part in a La Liga training clinic at Espanyol’s training ground, play against Spanish teams and visit one of Barcelona’s iconic football stadiums.

For some of the boys, this may be their first time travelling abroad with their teammates. For all of them, it’s an opportunity to experience something completely different, learn, grow and create memories together.

That’s why we’re fundraising.

Every contribution helps make this opportunity possible.

Whether you’re a parent, a football fan, a local business or simply someone who wants to support the boys, your help can make a real difference.
